# Package revision 2026-11-04a

This package revises `lpvs_tractability_2026-10-16c`, which is preserved unchanged. No computed result changed: every CSV and JSON results file is byte-identical to the October 16 version.

## Changes

1. **`c4_tables.py`: exact printing of award probabilities and fixed-cap exposures.**
   - With \(k\) of the twelve types accepting \(\bar V=25\) in a design, the award probability is \(1-(1-k/12)^n\) for the single offer (\(n=1\)), sequential offers and the auction. The exposure per opportunity of a single or sequential offer is that probability times \(\bar V\).
   - These cells are now printed from the exact fractions, rounded half up, after a check against the results files to \(10^{-9}\).
   - Floating-point results just below a rounding half had been printed as 0.937 (for \(15/16\)) and 18.7 (for \(75/4\)).
   - Seven cells changed in `c4_tables_main.tex` and seven in `c4_tables_appendix.tex`. No other cell changed.
2. **`c3_selection.py` and `c4_posted_offer.py`: description of bids in the run manifests.**
   - The previous entry was "truthful minimum acceptable caps (weakly dominant under C2)".
   - It now reads: "each sponsor bids its minimum acceptable cap (threshold bid); weakly dominant under the C2 second-price rule given assumption A6 (payoff at a positive threshold equals the outside option), which holds in the baseline with L = Lam = 0 (working paper, Remark 1); for L = -2 the results describe allocations under threshold bids, without an equilibrium claim".
   - The docstring of `c3_selection.py` was revised to match.
3. **Regenerated:**
   - `results_c3/manifest.json` and `results_c4/manifest.json`, by rerunning `c3_selection.py` and `c4_posted_offer.py`. They record the new code hashes and the revised bidding entry.
   - **Existing outputs listed in these manifests.** Their `outputs` lists also record the hashes of three existing files that were in the folder when the scripts ran. `c3_exit_violation_dates.csv`, written by `c3_exit_diagnostic.py`, appears in `results_c3/manifest.json`. `c4_sequential.csv` and `c4_access_rules.csv`, written by `c4_sequential.py`, appear in `results_c4/manifest.json`. `c3_selection.py` and `c4_posted_offer.py` do not generate these files. Every hash present in both the old and new lists is unchanged.
   - `c4_tables_main.tex` and `c4_tables_appendix.tex`, from `c4_tables.py`.
   - `manifest_all.json`, with the hashes of all files in this version.

4. **`c4_tables.py`: bid qualification in the termination-penalty table notes.**
   - The note of the per-opportunity table (`tab:c4opppen`, Table D14 of the working paper) adds: "Bids are minimum acceptable caps. The sensitivity exercise reports allocations under threshold bids, without establishing an equilibrium."
   - The notes of `tab:c4awardpen`, `tab:c4arpen` and `tab:c4decpen` (Tables D15–D17) add: "Bids as in Table~\ref{tab:c4opppen}: allocations under threshold bids, without an established equilibrium."
   - This matches the working paper. Only these four notes changed in `c4_tables_appendix.tex`; `c4_tables_main.tex` and every table body are unchanged.
